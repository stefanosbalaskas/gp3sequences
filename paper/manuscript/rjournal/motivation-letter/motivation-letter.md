# Motivation letter

Dear Editors of The R Journal,

We intend to submit the article **gp3sequences: Transparent, Reproducible,
and Auditable Analysis of Ordered Categorical Sequences in R**.

The article presents an R package that integrates validation, explicit
preparation, descriptive and inferential sequence methods, machine-readable
diagnostics, guarded interoperability, and provenance comparison for ordered
categorical sequences stored in ordinary long-format data frames.

The paper's intended contribution is workflow integration and auditability,
not replacement of established specialist packages. A primary-source-backed
comparison defines the boundaries between gp3sequences and TraMineR, seqHMM,
arulesSequences, WeightedCluster, ClickClust, PST, and march.

The submission provides reproducible code, deterministic example data,
generated tables and figures, and an explicit software-version boundary.
gp3sequences is publicly available through CRAN, while the evaluated
development snapshot is identified by package version and repository commit.
